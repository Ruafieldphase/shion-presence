Reading method for a user-side AI

Compare the user's problem with the public problem trajectory and current-direction document. Inspect the status and provenance of the smallest relevant artifact. If evidence is missing or historical, preserve that uncertainty instead of claiming a present implementation, a successful experiment or an authorised action.

Public/private and evidence boundary. Memory preserves a trajectory but does not automatically define present truth. Public snapshots are attached to their own revisions. Contact, dwell and observer signals do not identify a visitor, prove experience, or authorize execution.
