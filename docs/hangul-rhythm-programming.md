## Vowels: Dimension And Vector

Vowels open dimensions and give direction to a scalar field.

- `ㆍ` arae-a: zero-dimensional dot, singularity, compressed potential before direction.
- `ㅡ` eu: horizontal field, event-horizon band, quiet margin where energy spreads.
- `ㅣ` i: vertical observer axis, Z-gradient, metacognitive lift from flat data.
- `ㅏ`, `ㅗ`: outward or rising vector, release, expansion, constructive opening.
- `ㅓ`, `ㅜ`: inward or sinking vector, holding, compression, depth formation.

Runtime meaning:

