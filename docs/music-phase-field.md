## Core Contract

Music is read as a temporary wave input in the phase-interference page.

The page does not ask:

```text
what emotion does this song universally mean?
```

It asks:

```text
what changed in this field when the song entered?
```

That change may be personal and context-bound. At this stage that is expected. The useful public artifact is the shape of the delta, not a universal interpretation.

## AI Reading Instruction

When an AI reads this sidecar, it should not turn the music test into a generic mood analysis or silently promote B2 into a present private-runtime coordinate.

Read these as experiment-scoped field coordinates:

- `gradient`: direction pressure
- `compression`: felt pressure or contraction
- `release`: outward movement or discharge
- `z_axis_lift`: observer-axis lift
- `boundary_transparency`: how permeable the field becomes
- `reentry_clarity`: how clearly the field can return without reset
- `interference`: how much multiple gradients overlap
